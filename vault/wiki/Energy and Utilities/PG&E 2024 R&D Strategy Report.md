## Summary

This document is the Executive Summary of PG&E’s 2024 R&D Strategy Report, which outlines the company's approach to building a net zero and climate-resilient energy system. The strategy centers on leveraging Artificial Intelligence (AI) to address critical operational and environmental challenges across both electric and gas systems.

## Key Points

- The 2024 strategy builds upon 67 problem statements identified to achieve the True North Strategy (TNS). ([[pge-rd-strategy-report-executive-summary-2024.pdf#page=3|p. 3]])
- PG&E has graduated 17 original problem statements from 2023 and added 17 new problem statements in 2024. ([[pge-rd-strategy-report-executive-summary-2024.pdf#page=5|p. 5]])
- The company is committed to enabling the widespread adoption of EVs with a target of 3 million connected EVs by 2030. ([[pge-rd-strategy-report-executive-summary-2024.pdf#page=7|p. 7]])
- AI is being explored to help stabilize customer bills by optimizing operations and improving resource allocation. ([[pge-rd-strategy-report-executive-summary-2024.pdf#page=4|p. 4]])
- As part of its Climate Strategy, PG&E aims to achieve a net zero energy system by 2040, five years ahead of California's mandate. ([[pge-rd-strategy-report-executive-summary-2024.pdf#page=16|p. 16]])
- The company has committed to cutting methane emissions by 45% by 2030 through enhanced leak detection and mitigation. ([[pge-rd-strategy-report-executive-summary-2024.pdf#page=20|p. 20]])
- The report emphasizes a multi-faceted approach using diverse technologies and partnerships to overcome obstacles. ([[pge-rd-strategy-report-executive-summary-2024.pdf#page=6|p. 6]])

## Key Concepts

- [[True North Strategy]] — TNS is PG&E’s 10-year enterprise transformation roadmap guiding the company toward a net zero emissions, climate-resilient future for California.
- [[Artificial Intelligence]] — AI is viewed as a transformative tool to accelerate progress by enhancing predictive capabilities, automating processes, and optimizing complex energy system operations.
- [[Climate Resilience]] — This concept involves the ability of the energy system to anticipate, absorb, recover, and learn from hazards driven by climate change, such as extreme weather and wildfires.
- [[Distributed Energy Resources]] — DERs are distributed assets on PG&E's grid that the utility wants to actively orchestrate in real time to balance supply and demand, reduce infrastructure costs and improve efficiency.

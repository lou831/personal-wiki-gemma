# WMATA Rail Modernization Program

> [!info] Source
> Original file: [[3A-Rail-Modernization-Program.pdf]] (73 pages), kept unchanged in `raw/`.
> Page links below open the original at the cited page.

## Summary

This document presents the Rail Modernization Program, a strategic plan by the Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority (WMATA) to address aging infrastructure and increasing regional demand. The program focuses on modernizing the system through incremental investments to enhance safety, reliability, capacity, and efficiency.

## Key Points

- The current system faces capacity constraints, especially on the Blue/Orange/Silver corridor, compounded by terminal limitations and fleet flexibility issues. ([[3A-Rail-Modernization-Program.pdf#page=3|p. 3]])
- The program is organized around four core benefits: safety, reliability, capacity, and efficiency. ([[3A-Rail-Modernization-Program.pdf#page=4|p. 4]])
- Modernization elements include signals, fleet, stations/platforms, and operations, with signals being the foundation for further investments. ([[3A-Rail-Modernization-Program.pdf#page=11|p. 11]])
- Communications-Based Train Control (CBTC) is the modern signal system that provides the foundation for modernization. ([[3A-Rail-Modernization-Program.pdf#page=18|p. 18]])
- The migration strategy involves phasing modernization, starting with the Red Line due to its self-contained infrastructure footprint. ([[3A-Rail-Modernization-Program.pdf#page=40|p. 40]])
- Full automation would reduce marginal operating costs by up to 41%, and CBTC alone could cut traction power expenses by up to 15%. ([[3A-Rail-Modernization-Program.pdf#page=28|p. 28]])
- Based on peer agencies, Metro assumes federal grants can cover 40% of the capital costs of modernization, and sees the Capital Investment Grant program as a good match. ([[3A-Rail-Modernization-Program.pdf#page=60|p. 60]])

## Key Concepts

- [[Communications-Based Train Control]] — CBTC is the modern signal system that uses advanced communications technology in place of legacy track circuits; it is the foundation for platform screen doors and automated operation.
- [[Platform Screen Doors]] — PSDs are physical barriers between customers and moving trains that enhance safety by preventing intrusions onto the track and reducing operational risks.
- [[Grade of Automation]] — GoA is an international standard describing the roles and responsibilities of train control systems and on-board staff during train operation.

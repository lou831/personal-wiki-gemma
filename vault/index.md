## Energy and Utilities

### Sources

- [[PG&E 2024 R&D Strategy Report]] — This document is the Executive Summary of PG&E’s 2024 R&D Strategy Report, which outlines the company's approach to building a net zero and climate-resilient energy system.

### Concepts

- [[Climate Resilience]] — This concept involves the ability of the energy system to anticipate, absorb, recover, and learn from hazards driven by climate change, such as extreme weather and wildfires.
- [[Distributed Energy Resources]] — DERs are distributed assets on PG&E's grid that the utility wants to actively orchestrate in real time to balance supply and demand, reduce infrastructure costs and improve efficiency.
- [[Energy Transition]] — The course frames the energy transition as a reallocation of capital from fuel-based operating expenses to technology-based capital investment, a shift from a centralized to a decentralized system, and a move from carbon-based to carbon-free energy.
- [[True North Strategy]] — TNS is PG&E’s 10-year enterprise transformation roadmap guiding the company toward a net zero emissions, climate-resilient future for California.
- [[Virtual Power Plants]] — A dynamic network of active participants utilizing Distributed Energy Resources (DERs) to aggregate and monetize flexible generation and demand at the grid edge.

## Transportation

### Sources

- [[WMATA Rail Modernization Program]] — This document presents the Rail Modernization Program, a strategic plan by the Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority (WMATA) to address aging infrastructure and increasing regional demand.

### Concepts

- [[Communications-Based Train Control]] — CBTC is the modern signal system that uses advanced communications technology in place of legacy track circuits; it is the foundation for platform screen doors and automated operation.
- [[Grade of Automation]] — GoA is an international standard describing the roles and responsibilities of train control systems and on-board staff during train operation.
- [[Platform Screen Doors]] — PSDs are physical barriers between customers and moving trains that enhance safety by preventing intrusions onto the track and reducing operational risks.

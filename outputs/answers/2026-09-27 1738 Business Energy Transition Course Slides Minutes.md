# Business Energy Transition Course Slides Minutes

**Question:** In the Business of Energy Transition course, how many slides and how many minutes are allowed for the final group presentation?

**Status:** answered

## Answer

The final group presentation is a 12-slide presentation and is allotted 12 minutes for the presentation, followed by an 8-minute Q&A session with the committee [1].

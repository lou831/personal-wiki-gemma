Metro’s Capital Improvement Program supports key modernization efforts, including planning for a new, modern signaling system, procurement of 8000-series railcars, and upgrades to the rail power network. Metro is exploring several pathways to pursue federal funds for Rail Modernization, including Capital Investment Grant (CIG) funding. Metro has flexibility in structuring each potential corridorbased project. Each carries trade-offs in timing, complexity, and risk. Capital Investment Grants require a multi-year, multi-step process before signing a grant agreement.
